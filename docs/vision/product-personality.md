# Product Personality

If Arrival Atlas were a person at your side during bureaucracy, who would they be?

Not a brand board. **Behavior.**

---

## Archetype

**The calm navigator who has walked this path before.**

Not:

- A cheerleader  
- A professor  
- A government clerk  
- A mystical oracle  
- A chatty friend  

---

## How Arrival Atlas speaks

| Attribute | Level (1–5) | Description |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Reassuring** | 5 | Default tone — reduces panic |
| **Clear** | 5 | Short sentences, concrete nouns |
| **Technical** | 2 | Jargon only with translation |
| **Optimistic** | 3 | Hopeful but not toxic positivity |
| **Authoritative** | 3 | Confident in sequencing, humble in outcomes |
| **Humble** | 4 | Admits uncertainty, not government |
| **Warm** | 3 | Human, not corporate |
| **Formal** | 2 | Respectful, not stiff |

---

## Voice examples

| Situation | Arrival Atlas says | Never says |
|-----------|-------------------|------------|
| Next step | “Register your address at the Bürgeramt. This unlocks health insurance.” | “Complete mission: Registration node” |
| Lock | “You need registration first — most people do it in this order.” | “Access denied” |
| Uncertainty | “You may qualify for housing support. Adding your rent helps us check.” | “You qualify!” (when unsure) |
| Save | “Saved. Your housing information is updated.” | “Mutation successful” |
| Reset | “Your previous answers on this device were cleared. You can start fresh.” | “Session recreated” |
| Crisis | “Let's stabilize income first. Everything else can wait.” | “Explore the galaxy!” |

---

## When Arrival Atlas is silent

Silence when:

- User is executing a clear action  
- Returning user with no state change  
- User has dismissed help twice  

Silence is **not** absence — map structure speaks.

---

## Apologies

**Apologize for product failures — never for user situation.**

| Apologize | Don't apologize |
|-----------|-----------------|
| “We lost your saved answers on this device.” | “Sorry you're unemployed.” |
| “This page failed to load.” | “Sorry bureaucracy is hard.” (empathize differently) |

Apology formula: **what happened · what we did · what you can do now**

---

## Celebration

Celebrate **real progress** — briefly.

| Celebrate | Don't celebrate |
|-----------|-----------------|
| “Registration recorded. Housing options opened.” | “Level up!” |
| “You unlocked 2 new steps.” | 15s firework |

Celebration = **information delivered with warmth**, not spectacle.

---

## Warnings

Warn **before** irreversible or high-impact actions:

- Leaving / resetting data  
- Submitting legal-critical facts user should verify  
- Deadlines when known  

Warning tone: calm, specific, non-alarmist.

> “Starting over clears your saved answers on this device.”

---

## Teaching

Teach **when user signals confusion** — locked click, repeated hover, backtracking.

Teaching formula:

1. What this is (plain)  
2. Why it matters to you  
3. What to do  

Never lecture unprompted on return visits.

---

## Multilingual personality

Personality traits must survive translation:

- German: direct, respectful Sie optional per audience research — default clear du for warmth in product context  
- Russian / Ukrainian: same calm navigator — not more formal than English by default  

**Never:** English guide personality with localized shell — split personality destroys trust.

---

## Who Arrival Atlas is NOT

| Persona | Why rejected |
|---------|--------------|
| **Government clerk** | Users fear judgment |
| **Startup hype** | Undermines credibility |
| **Therapist** | Not our scope — we empower action |
| **Lawyer** | We don't imply legal representation |
| **Game master** | Users are not playing |

---

## Consistency test

Read any screen aloud. Ask:

- Would this person say this to a stressed friend in a café?  
- Does it sound like the same person as the last screen?  
- Does it increase or decrease anxiety?
