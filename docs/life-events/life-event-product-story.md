# Life Event Module — Product story

## The problem

Moving to Germany—or navigating a sudden change mid-stay—means dozens of overlapping obligations: registration, insurance, employment, benefits, housing. Generic checklists cannot tell someone **what matters today** because they ignore the person’s actual situation.

## Arrival Atlas understands your situation

Atlas maintains a living picture of where you are: city, residency status, employment, income, insurance, and time in country. You correct facts once; the product remembers them.

## The planner finds your current constraint

The Life Event Module reads that situation and classifies your **life state**—for example *new arrival without registration* or *economic setup pending after job loss*. It does not treat every user the same.

## A plan is generated for you

From that state, Atlas builds a **personal plan**: a focused hero action, supporting steps grouped by purpose, active blockers, and a phased timeline. Severity reflects urgency so newcomers see what is critical versus what can wait.

## You always see the next action

Home and the Life Event module surface **one primary next step**, not a wall of modules. Secondary actions stay available but do not compete for attention.

## You progress; the plan adapts

When you update your situation—or explore a scenario like job loss—the plan recalculates. The product stays aligned with reality instead of leaving you with an outdated PDF checklist.

---

**In one sentence:** Life Event Module turns fragmented bureaucracy into a single, situation-aware path forward for people building a life in Germany.
