# Life Event Module — Executive summary

**Audience:** investors, stakeholders, partners  
**Length:** one page

---

## What it is

The **Life Event Module** is Arrival Atlas’s situation-aware planning layer for people living in Germany. It reads what we know about a user’s residency, work, income, housing, and insurance—and produces a **personal plan** with one clear next action, supporting steps, and visible blockers.

It is a core product surface, not a side feature or static content library.

## Why it matters

Germany’s administrative path is fragmented across federal, state, and municipal systems. Newcomers and long-term residents alike face the same failure mode: **too many tasks, no sense of order**. Life Event Module reduces anxiety and drop-off by answering one question repeatedly: *“What should I do next, given my situation?”*

## How it differs from a checklist

| Checklist | Life Event Module |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Same for everyone | Adapts to classified life state |
| Flat task list | Prioritized hero action + phased timeline |
| Static PDF or blog post | Updates when the user’s situation changes |
| No sense of urgency | Severity and blockers show what cannot wait |
| Modules siloed | Connects registration, insurance, work, and benefits in one plan |

## Who it helps in Germany

- **New arrivals** needing registration and first-week survival steps  
- **Workers facing job loss** who must stabilize income and access support  
- **Households exploring benefits** such as Wohngeld without navigating blind  
- **Established residents** preparing for the next life transition  

## Proof in demo

Four canonical personas load in one click during development demos—each backed by real planner output. A stakeholder can go from zero context to a convincing walkthrough in **under ten minutes**.

## Strategic value

Life Event Module is the **differentiated core** of Arrival Atlas: it turns modular knowledge into personalized guidance. Partners see a product that respects German institutional complexity while remaining approachable to users who did not grow up inside the system.
